Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Mishkah is a personal Qur'an reading app. At each appointed time it presents a reading and holds your phone until it is done. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Mishkah has no account, no login, and no servers of its own. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. Everything the app remembers stays on your device.

Location

With your permission, Mishkah reads your device location to calculate the daily prayer times (Fajr, Maghrib, Isha) that schedule your readings. This calculation happens entirely on your device. Your location is not sent to any server, is not stored remotely, and is never shared with anyone. A single approximate position, refreshed at most once a day, is kept locally so the app works offline. You can revoke location access at any time in your device settings.

What is stored on your device

  • Your reading completions, streak, and daily progress.
  • Your settings: which readings are enabled, calculation method, reading text size.
  • Any duas you add to your routine.

This information lives only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes it (subject to any device backup you have enabled through Google, which is controlled by you, not by Mishkah).

Notifications

Mishkah uses notifications to remind you shortly before a reading is due and while a reading is pending. These are generated on your device.

Data collection and sharing

Mishkah does not collect analytics, does not use advertising, does not use tracking technologies, and contains no third-party advertising or analytics software. No data is shared with third parties.

Content sources

The Qur'anic text, translation (Saheeh International), and tafsir (Ibn Kathir, abridged) are bundled from quran.com. The daily supplications are from Munajat al-Faqir (Sayyid Mukhtar-ud-Din Shah, published by Dar-ul-Eeman). This content is included with the app; using it does not send any information about you anywhere.

Children

Mishkah is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
